# Code of Conduct

## Expected behavior

Participants in this project are expected to:

- communicate directly and respectfully
- focus criticism on ideas, code, data, and decisions, not people
- assume good faith unless there is clear evidence otherwise
- be explicit about uncertainty, limitations, and tradeoffs
- keep discussions relevant to the project

## Unacceptable behavior

The following are not acceptable in project spaces:

- harassment, intimidation, or personal attacks
- discriminatory or hateful language
- deliberate disruption, bad-faith argument, or persistent derailing
- publishing private information without permission
- repeated low-signal spam, abuse, or hostile escalation

## Maintainer responsibilities

Project maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove comments, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that do not meet these standards. They may also temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the project.

## Reporting

If you experience or observe unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainer through the repository owner account on GitHub:

- https://github.com/DiogoRibeiro7

Include links, screenshots, or other context where possible.

## Enforcement

Enforcement decisions are at the discretion of the maintainer and will be made with the goal of keeping the project constructive, technically useful, and safe to participate in.
